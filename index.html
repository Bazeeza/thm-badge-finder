How badges work

Four ways a badge is awarded

  1. Single room. Finish every task and the badge appears within minutes. Blue, OhSINT, Hammer.
  2. Module badge. Attached to the last room of a module, but it checks the whole module. Finish the earlier rooms first. Networking Nerd, Metasploitable, every SOC Level 1 module badge.
  3. Series badge. Enrol from Learn, then Practice, then Series and complete every room from the series page. WindCorp, Overpassed, Pentester Tools.
  4. Platform badge. No room at all: SOC Simulator scenarios, weekly leagues, streaks, the mobile app, seasonal events.

Old room, new room

When TryHackMe refreshes a room or a path, the badge moves with it. Wireshark now comes from the SOC Level 1 Wireshark rooms rather than Wireshark 101; "Phishing" was renamed Phish Hunter; Hash Cracker needs both Crack The Hash rooms. Cards list the current trigger first.

Didn't get it?

  • Open the room and confirm 100% complete, including tasks with no questions.
  • For module and series badges, open the module page and confirm every room is ticked.
  • Refresh your profile after a few minutes; badges aren't always instant.
  • Still missing? Ask TryHackMe support to re-sync your completions.

Found a mistake? New badge?

Rooms get renamed and retired, and TryHackMe ships new badges without a changelog. The fastest fix is to open an issue with the badge name and the room link, or edit data/badges.js and send a pull request. Cards marked needs verification are the ones to confirm first — if you earned one, tell us which room triggered it.
